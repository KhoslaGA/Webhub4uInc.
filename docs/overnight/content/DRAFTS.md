## 1. Five things to include in your website brief

Format: seven-slide 1080×1350 carousel.

1. **A better website starts with a clearer brief.** Five things to bring to the first conversation.
2. **01 / Who is it for?** Name your main customer. What brought them here, and what do they already know?
3. **02 / What should they do?** Choose one useful action: ask a question, request an estimate, book a visit or complete a task.
4. **03 / What do they need to know?** List the questions people ask before choosing you. Build the page around useful answers.
5. **04 / What happens next?** Decide who receives an enquiry, where it goes and how your team follows up.
6. **05 / What would success look like?** Pick something observable: a completed task, a delivered enquiry or fewer repeated steps. Establish a baseline before claiming improvement.
7. **Start with what needs to work better.** You don’t need every answer. Bring your website, your customers and one problem to discuss. webhub4u.com/contact

## 2. Does every city need its own service page?

Feed copy: **Change the city name. Does the page still say the same thing?** If yes, improve the page before multiplying it. A useful local page reflects actual service coverage, relevant examples and questions specific to that place.

Caption: A city name alone is not a content strategy. Start with the service people need and the information that helps them decide.
